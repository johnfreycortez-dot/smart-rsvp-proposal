Overview

How it works, from new event to thank-you

Six steps. You only do the first three; the rest happens by itself. Tap a step to see it.

You

Add the event in Google Sheets

Couple, date, venues, deadline and the guest list. Pick a layout, colours and fonts for the RSVP page.

See your Sheet → You

Tick “Send Links to Couple”

The details are checked, then the couple gets their private dashboard, RSVP link and QR codes by email.

Try the tick box → You

Keep designing in Canva as usual. The RSVP button now opens the Smart RSVP page instead of a Google Form.

Confirmations, reminders, the event countdown emails, closing on the deadline and the thank-you after.

Finds names even with typos

“andrea santo” still finds Andrea Santos. If two invitations match, it asks for the full name.

Seats set by you, per group

A family of four sees exactly four names. No plus-ones can be added by guests.

Accept or decline per person

One tap per name. “Partly attending” is counted correctly for the caterer.

Role and a personal note

Principal Sponsors, 18 Roses, Maid of Honor and others see their role and a message only they get.

Table, calendar and directions

After replying: their table number, an Add to calendar button, venues and Google Maps.

Countdown and “When & where”

A live countdown to the day and the venues with map buttons, right on the RSVP page.

Change their answer

Guests can come back and update their reply until the deadline. The latest answer counts.

Find my table on the day

A QR code at the venue entrance opens “Find my table”. Guests type their name and see where they sit.

For the couple or celebrant

A private dashboard with the live headcount

This is the page the couple opens from their link. Try adding a guest, editing one, sending a reminder or opening the seating list. Replies you make above appear here too.

Each couple's link is private and only shows their own event.

Live headcount

Seats reserved, attending, declined and still waiting, with a progress bar and a replies-over-time chart.

Add, edit and remove guests

Couples can add their own guests. Group name, main guest, and an email or mobile are required. Duplicate names are blocked.

Message guests

One button sends a gentle “please RSVP” to everyone who hasn't replied. Announcements go to attending guests or everyone.

Seating and caterer list

Round tables with a dot per guest (gold for VIPs), the final headcount, and who still has no table.

Filter, search, print, download

Filter by reply, search names, print the list, or download it for Excel or Google Sheets.

Updates by itself

Every reply shows up right away. Couples also get a short email for each new reply.

Emails

Seven emails that go out by themselves

Each one uses the event's colours. Every email to a guest also sends a copy (CC) to the couple, and every type can be switched off in Settings.

Automatic

What happens by itself, and when

Press “Try it” to fast-forward the sample wedding and watch the Outbox.

No accidental sends

The couple's links only go out after you tick the box, the details pass a check, and 10 minutes pass. Untick to cancel.

Each email has its own switch

Confirmations, alerts, reminders, thank-yous and the couple's CC copies can each be turned off in Settings.

No double invitations

The same name can't be added twice to one event, whether by you or by the couple.

Private links

Each couple's dashboard has its own secret key. The guest page never shows other guests' details.

Optional privacy check

Turn on “last 4 digits of mobile” so only the right guest can open an invitation.

Everything is logged

Every reply is saved with the time in the Responses tab, including changed answers.
