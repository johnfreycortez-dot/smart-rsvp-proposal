Why change

Your invitations are beautiful. The Google Form behind them isn't.

  • ✕Guests can type in extra names.
  • ✕Looks plain, nothing like your invitation.
  • ✕Couples count replies by hand and chase late guests.

Smart RSVP proposed

  • ✓Only guests on the couple's list can respond.

  • ✓Same colors, fonts and feel as each invitation.
